<h1 class="home-intro__title">Fulong Li</h1>
<p class="home-intro__lede">I am a power electronics researcher and engineer based in the UK. My work spans power semiconductor characterisation and loss modelling, converter design and control, and the control and energy management of DC microgrids and hybrid energy systems.</p>
<dl class="home-facts">
<div><dt>2022–2025</dt><dd>Power Electronics Engineer, Compound Semiconductor Applications (CSA) Catapult: device characterisation and reliability test rigs, ANN-based SiC MOSFET modelling, converter design, and a 3 MW marine fuel-cell powertrain study.</dd></div>
<div><dt>2019–2022</dt><dd>Research Associate, Loughborough University: hierarchical control and energy management for plug-and-play low-voltage DC microgrids.</dd></div>
<div><dt>2016–2019</dt><dd>PhD, Aston University: <a href="https://publications.aston.ac.uk/id/eprint/46135/"><em>Control Strategies for Low Voltage DC Microgrids</em></a>. Visiting research at the University of Waterloo, Zhejiang University and DC Systems BV.</dd></div>

## Current focus

I am building open, inspectable tools for AI-assisted power electronics design: device libraries, loss and thermal models, and optimisation workflows that engineers and learners can reproduce. The public repositories are on [GitHub](https://github.com/FulongLi); the [AIPE page](/research/aipe/) explains how they relate to my earlier research.
