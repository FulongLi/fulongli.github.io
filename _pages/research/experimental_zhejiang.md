During visiting research at Zhejiang University, I worked with an existing **50 kW DC microgrid platform**, giving experimental context at a larger scale than my low-voltage laboratory testbeds.

## Connecting a DC Microgrid to the AC Grid

A DC microgrid usually exchanges power with the AC grid through a three-phase voltage-source converter. This AC–DC interface regulates the DC bus while drawing or returning power through the grid, so its control strongly influences the stability and power balance of the whole microgrid.

## Platform and My Work

The platform operates at up to 50 kW and includes a three-phase voltage-source converter interface between the AC grid and the DC microgrid. I worked with this platform and its AC–DC interface during the visit. Detailed results from this work are not published here.
