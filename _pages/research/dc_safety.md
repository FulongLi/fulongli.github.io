## Engineering problem

DC distribution changes two safety assumptions built into AC practice. DC fault current has no natural zero crossing, so interrupting it is harder; and the body's response to DC current differs from its response to 50/60 Hz AC, so AC touch-voltage limits do not carry over directly. Low-voltage DC buildings and microgrids need protection and earthing schemes designed with both in mind.

## What I did

As a visiting PhD student at **DC Systems BV** in the Netherlands (July–August 2017), I evaluated human-safety issues related to DC electric shock and investigated DC protection strategies. The same year I presented *Capacitive Grounding for DC Microgrids* at the first RDC2MT project workshop at TU Delft (7 September 2017).
