## Modified MPPT and Integrated Active Power Control

**Overview:** During my early PhD research, I investigated a modified maximum power point tracking (MPPT) algorithm with integrated active power control for PV–battery systems.

**My contribution:** Researched PV control and its integration into DC microgrids; the publication documents the algorithm and study.

**Related publications:** [“A modified MPPT algorithm with integrated active power control for PV-battery systems,” IEEE ICRERA 2016](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7884432).
