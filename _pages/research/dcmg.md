## Overview

My DC microgrid research addresses hierarchical control, converter stability, power sharing, and coordination of renewable sources and battery storage.

<img class="research-hero-image" src="/images/research/generated/dc-microgrids.png" alt="DC microgrid technical illustration" />

## Hierarchical Control and DC Bus Signalling

**Overview:** DC bus signalling supports interaction between sources and energy storage within a hierarchical control scheme.

**My contribution:** Developed and investigated DC bus signalling control methods for source and storage coordination.

**Related publications:**

- [“Active DC bus signaling control method for coordinating multiple energy storage devices in DC microgrid,” IEEE ICDCM 2017](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8001048).
- [“A Dual-Window DC Bus Interacting Method for DC Microgrids Hierarchical Control Scheme,” IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy 2020](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8645723).
- [“Review of DC Bus Signalling Control Methods in DC Microgrids,” IEEE PEAC 2022](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9959577).

## Primary Converter Control and Stability Analysis

**Overview:** Primary converter control links local dynamics to DC bus stability, including Boost converter and PV control; fuel-cell control is a related topic for later enrichment.

**My contribution:** Investigated Boost converter stability and terminal-capacitor compensation methods for DC microgrids.

**Related publications:**

- [“Novel Passive Controller Design for Enhancing Boost Converter Stability in DC Microgrid Applications,” IEEE JESTPE 2021](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9391691).
- [“Terminal Capacitor Compensation Based Stability Design for DC Microgrids,” IEEE ICDCM 2019](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9232923).

Converter details are organised under [Non-Isolated / Bidirectional DC–DC Converters](/research/nonisolated_converters/).

## Droop Control and Power Sharing

**Overview:** My work addresses line-resistance effects, DC bus voltage variation, and sharing accuracy in distributed DC systems.

**My contribution:** Developed and investigated local control and compensation methods for power sharing.

**Related publications:**

- [“A Low-pass Filter Method to Suppress the Voltage Variations Caused by Introducing Droop Control in DC Microgrids,” IEEE ECCE 2018](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8557455).
- [“Virtual Negative Cable Resistance for Power Sharing Accuracy Enhancement in DC Microgrids,” IEEE ISIE 2019](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8781426).
- [“Comprehensive local control design for eliminating line resistance effect on power sharing degradation in DC microgrids,” IET Power Electronics 2022](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1049/pel2.12208).

See [Battery Energy Storage Systems](/research/battery_storage/) for battery power-sharing and plug-and-play control projects.
