## Engineering problem

Wide-bandgap devices and new packaging approaches, such as power devices embedded inside a PCB, have to show that they survive the environmental and electrical stresses of their application before they can be adopted. Accelerated stress tests expose specific failure mechanisms: humidity-driven leakage and corrosion under reverse bias, thermo-mechanical fatigue of interconnects under temperature cycling, and breakdown of package insulation.

## Objective and scope

Provide reliability test capability and test results for SiC and GaN devices and modules in three industry-funded projects at CSA Catapult:

| Project | Devices | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| P3EP: Pre-packaged Power Devices for PCB Embedded Power Electronics | GaN devices, including PCB-embedded devices | Temperature cycling of PCB-embedded devices; H3TRB |
| @FutureBEV: Accelerated Technologies for Future Battery Electric Vehicles | SiC and GaN devices | Insulation testing, H3TRB |
| ASSiST: Advanced SiC-based Solid State Transformer | SiC modules | H3TRB |

## My contribution

I designed and commissioned the test rig and carried out the tests listed above.

## Method

- **H3TRB** (high humidity, high temperature, reverse bias) holds devices in blocking state inside a climatic chamber and monitors leakage current over the exposure. Common qualification conditions are 85 °C and 85 % relative humidity.
- **Temperature cycling** moves devices repeatedly between temperature extremes; parameters measured before and after cycling reveal degradation of die attach and interconnects.
- **Insulation testing** applies high voltage between isolated parts of a package or module and checks for leakage or breakdown.
