My solid-state transformer work focused on technology assessment and architectural review: how power devices, converter stages and control choices fit together at system level.

## What a Solid-State Transformer Is

A solid-state transformer replaces a bulky line-frequency transformer with power-electronic conversion stages around a compact high-frequency isolation transformer. Because the conversion is actively controlled, it can also regulate voltage, support bidirectional power flow and provide DC connections for renewable generation and storage. Its feasibility at medium voltage depends strongly on high-voltage semiconductor devices, which is why SiC technology is central to it.

## Architecture and Technology Assessment

I reviewed high-voltage SiC device technology and assessed solid-state transformer converter topologies, control approaches and applications. The work connects my device characterisation background to isolated conversion at system level. Design and simulation references are collected in the [solid-state transformer repository](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Solid-State-Transformer).
