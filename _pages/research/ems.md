This energy management research connects generation and storage models to operating decisions, from rule-based microgrid coordination to battery scheduling and marine hybrid powertrains.

## DC Microgrid Energy Management

The [rule-based DC microgrid study](/research/dcmg/rule-based-ems/) coordinates converter roles, battery charging and PV limitation through bus signalling. Its operating rules cover planned grid/island handover and surplus-generation control.

## Battery Energy Scheduling and Optimisation

Research at the University of Waterloo incorporates battery degradation into GAMS-based microgrid optimisation, linking scheduling choices to battery use. [Read the battery-degradation EMS](/research/mgapp/battery-degradation-ems/) or the [IEEE PESGM 2020 paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9281580).

## Marine Hybrid Powertrains

The [3MW marine fuel-cell project](/research/3mw-marine-fuel-cell/), led by the author, connected vessel duty cycles to battery sizing, dispatch and heat generation. The related [thermal evaluation](/research/sino-ofh/) examines the enclosure environment around the equipment.
