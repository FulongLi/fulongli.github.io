## Overview

AIPE is the entry point for my AI-assisted power engineering work. Project descriptions live in their relevant engineering categories; the links below connect device models, converter optimisation, and engineering tools.

## AI-Assisted Semiconductor Device Modelling

**Overview:** Artificial neural networks model semiconductor characteristics for engineering calculations.

**My contribution:** Technical lead for the collaboration with Sumitomo Heavy Industries and Cardiff University, connecting the experimental platform, ANN loss and thermal models, and converter-level assessment.

**Outcome / evidence:** [ANN-based SiC MOSFET modelling](/research/mosfet_ann_modelling/), including WP2 device and thermal results, WP3 buck-converter experiments up to 3 kW, and a separate reproducible public-code example.

## Multi-Objective Converter Optimisation

**Overview:** Component models and optimisation expose loss–volume and efficiency trade-offs.

**My contribution:** Developed the converter design workflow through collaboration with Cardiff University.

**Outcome / evidence:** [Converter Design Optimisation](/research/converter_optimization/) contains the technical resources and calculated results.

## Engineering Design Agents

**Overview:** AI-assisted engineering design agents connect modelling and optimisation workflows.
