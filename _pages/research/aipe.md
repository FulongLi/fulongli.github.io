AIPE connects my work on device modelling, converter optimisation and open engineering tools. The aim is to make engineering data and models useful within an inspectable design workflow.

## AI-Assisted Semiconductor Device Modelling

I developed the modelling and assessment workflow linking fitted device characteristics to converter losses. The [SiC static ANN study](/research/semiconductor-device-modelling/static-ann/) explains the network branches, training and a buck-converter comparison up to 3 kW. [Semiconductor modelling overview](/research/mosfet_ann_modelling/).

## Multi-Objective Converter Optimisation

I proposed an integrated component-modelling and converter-design framework, then continued its development through AIPE. [Explore the design framework](/research/converter_optimization/).

## Engineering Design Agents

The [public converter optimisation repository](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Converter-Optimisation-Agent) connects component calculations, optimisation and surrogate modelling as a focused example of AI-assisted engineering tools.

[AIPE PCB Agent](/research/aipe-pcb-agent/) connects structured requirements to KiCad schematics, PCB layouts, checks and exports. The project page introduces its automation framework and a 5 kW SiC buck converter with separate power and DSP control boards.

[AIPE CAD Agent](/research/aipe-cad-agent/) builds parametric FreeCAD models of transformers, heat sinks, cold plates and converter assemblies, and checks them for interference, fit and clearance. The project page introduces its structure and the mechanical models of a 10 kW DAB converter.

[AIPE Sketcher](/research/aipe-sketcher/) turns circuit descriptions into SVG schematics and checks each drawing by reading its connections back from the wires. The project page introduces its structure and a dual-active-bridge schematic.
