Isolated DC–DC converters transfer power through a high-frequency transformer, providing galvanic isolation and a large voltage step in a compact stage. In my professional work I designed and simulated isolated converters for data-centre power applications, using LLC resonant and dual-active-bridge topologies.

## LLC Resonant Converter

I studied a 10 kW LLC resonant converter for a 750 V input and 50 V output, from resonant-tank design and parameter sweeps to closed-loop simulation.

[Read the LLC converter study](/research/isolated-converters/llc/)

## Dual Active Bridge (DAB)

A dual active bridge connects two full bridges through a transformer and its series inductance, controlling power flow in either direction through the phase shift between the bridges. My DAB work covered the design of isolated conversion for the same application area.
