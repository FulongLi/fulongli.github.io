My non-isolated converter work connects local control to DC microgrid operation, including source interfaces, bidirectional battery converters and Boost-converter stability.

<h2 id="bidirectional-buck-boost-converter">Bidirectional Half-Bridge Buck–Boost Converter</h2>
<span id="bidirectional-half-bridge-buckboost-converter"></span>

Bidirectional battery interfaces connect battery charging and discharging with DC bus regulation. The modular half-bridge prototype combines a power stage, sensing, isolated gate drives and DSP control. [Explore the converter architecture and ratings](/research/nonisolated-converters/bidirectional-buck-boost/).

## Buck / Boost Converter Control

I researched passive stabilisation of Boost interface converters. The filtered compensator suppresses oscillation while avoiding a steady-state voltage shift in the tested conditions. [Read the passive-control study](/research/dcmg/passive-control/) or the [IEEE JESTPE 2021 paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9391691).

[Public Buck and Boost converter resources](https://github.com/FulongLi/NonisolatedCoverters)
