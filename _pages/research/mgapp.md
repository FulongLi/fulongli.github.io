## 3 MW Marine Fuel-Cell Hybrid Powertrain

**Overview:** A 3 MW (three-megawatt) marine fuel-cell hybrid powertrain combining fuel-cell and battery energy storage technologies.

**My contribution:** Worked on system architecture, modelling, energy management strategy development, and related thermal-behaviour assessment.

**Outcome / evidence:** This public summary does not establish hardware validation or measured performance.

**GitHub / external resources:** [Existing public marine energy management resource](https://github.com/FulongLi/MicrogridEnergyManagementSystermDesign). The repository is a related resource; its contents should not be assumed to represent the full industrial project.
