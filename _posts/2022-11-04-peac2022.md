## Summary

I joined **IEEE PEAC 2022**, associated with Guangzhou, China, online rather than travelling to the venue. We presented our review paper, **"Review of DC Bus Signalling Control Methods in DC Microgrids"**, through a virtual poster presentation.

I was very happy that our contribution received a **Best Paper Award**. Although I participated remotely, the recognition made this conference a particularly positive memory and a moment to celebrate the work with my co-authors.
