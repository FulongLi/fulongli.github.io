## Summary

I participated online in the **IEEE PES General Meeting 2020**, which had originally been planned for Montreal, Canada. My contribution was:

> **"Energy Management System for DC Microgrids Considering Battery Degradation"**

The paper grew out of my visiting research at the **University of Waterloo**, working with **[Prof. Claudio A. Cañizares](https://uwaterloo.ca/scholar/ccanizar/home)**. It examined energy management in DC microgrids with battery degradation taken into account, considering the longer-term consequences of how batteries are operated.

---

## A Conference During the Pandemic

Unfortunately, the COVID-19 pandemic meant that I could not travel to Montreal. Instead, I recorded a short video to present the work remotely. Recorded presentations became a familiar part of academic life during that period, allowing us to share results even when meeting in person was not possible.

Overall, I remember the experience positively, but I was disappointed to miss the chance to return to Canada and see Montreal. I still hope to visit the city in person one day.
