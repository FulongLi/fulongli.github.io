## Summary

I attended the **IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE) 2018** in Portland, Oregon, where I presented a **poster** on our research related to voltage variations in droop-controlled DC microgrids.

This was my first visit to the United States, and Portland left me with very positive memories. Alongside the conference, I had time to explore the city and experience a little of life on the American West Coast.

## Exploring the City

One practical detail I appreciated was Oregon's lack of a general sales tax. I visited an Apple Store and bought an iPad during the trip. I also enjoyed trying local steak, another small but vivid memory of that first visit.

My accommodation was away from the downtown area, so I used public transport to explore different parts of the city. One evening, on the journey back, I missed my stop. I got off at the next one and ended up walking a long way to where I was staying.

## Finding My Own Impressions

Before the trip, I had heard plenty of warnings about safety in American cities, especially after dark. That evening, though, I felt reasonably comfortable on the walk and noticed other people out jogging at night.

It was only my own experience of one evening, but it reminded me how much first-hand experience matters. The stories we hear before visiting a place can shape our expectations, while being there gives us a chance to form a more personal and nuanced impression. For me, Portland was a very good start to travelling in the United States.
