AIPE (AI for Power Engineering) is the open engineering ecosystem I am building through AIPE Labs. It treats power-converter design as a coordinated, iterative optimisation process rather than a chain of separate tools. Specialist agents each own one engineering step: electrical sizing, circuit simulation, device and magnetics selection, thermal and field analysis, mechanical CAD and PCB layout. They exchange explicit inputs, assumptions, results and evidence through a shared Engineering State.

A workflow engine runs these steps as a checkpointed task graph. Results from later stages, such as magnetics or finite-element parameters, feed back into the circuit model, and every result is recorded against a verification gate. Software checks do not replace engineering review, measurement or hardware qualification, which remain separate gates. The first full benchmark is a 10 kW dual-active-bridge converter (800 V → 400 V, 100 kHz).
