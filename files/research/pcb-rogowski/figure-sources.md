# PCB Rogowski coil figure sources

## Drawn figures

- `/images/research/pcb-rogowski/development-timeline.svg` was drawn for the website on 29 October 2026 in the shared site palette. Its entries come from Fulong Li's 2022 review document, "Rogowski coil review in one table" (40 references), and his earlier development-timeline slide. Chattock (1887) and the Cooper (1963) attribution follow the standard citations of those works.
- `/images/research/pcb-rogowski/winding-shapes.svg` was drawn for the website on 29 October 2026. It schematically redraws the four patterns of Figure 5 in M. Koga, M. Tsukuda, K. Nakashima and I. Omura, "Application-specific micro Rogowski coil for power modules: design tool, novel coil pattern and demonstration," CIPS 2016 (open-access copy: http://hdl.handle.net/10228/5769). The maximum outside-current errors (7%, 10%, 26%, 218%), the 4% front-side value and the −1.1% to +0.7% sensitivity range quoted on the page are taken from Section 3.2 of that paper.

## Design figure

`/images/research/pcb-rogowski/coil-designs.png` places side by side the coil areas (left 198 pixels, full height) cropped from `images/RogT1_t.png` to `images/RogT5_t.png` in the [PCB-Rogowski-Coil repository](https://github.com/FulongLi/PCB-Rogowski-Coil) at commit `f1f8c6b` (CC BY 4.0, Fulong Li). The crops are not rescaled or recoloured; the white gaps between them are added. The pattern, return-line and outline labels follow each board's silkscreen.
