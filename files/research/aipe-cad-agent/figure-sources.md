# AIPE CAD Agent figure sources

## Example renders

The three renders are copied without cropping, recolouring or resizing from the [AIPE-CAD-Agent 10 kW DAB example](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-CAD-Agent/tree/main/examples/dab-10kw/renders) (commit `b81bf69`, 22 October 2026). They are headless matplotlib projections of the tessellated FreeCAD solids generated by the agent, 1540 × 1100 pixels.

| Published asset | Repository file |
| --- | --- |
| `/images/research/aipe-cad-agent/transformer-cad.png` | `examples/dab-10kw/renders/dab_transformer.png` |
| `/images/research/aipe-cad-agent/heatsink-cad.png` | `examples/dab-10kw/renders/dab_heatsink.png` |
| `/images/research/aipe-cad-agent/assembly-cad.png` | `examples/dab-10kw/renders/dab_10kw_assembly.png` |

Geometry, masses and check outcomes are taken from [the example README](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-CAD-Agent/blob/main/examples/dab-10kw/README.md). Workflow-run figures are taken from [the AIPE-Workflows DAB example](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Workflows/blob/main/examples/dab-10kw/README.md), accessed on 28 October 2026.
