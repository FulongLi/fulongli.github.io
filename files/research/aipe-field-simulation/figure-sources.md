# AIPE Field Simulation figure sources

## Example figures

The five figures are copied without cropping, recolouring or resizing from the [AIPE-Field-Simulation 10 kW DAB heat-sink example](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/tree/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/results/figures) (commit `63d2898`, 29 October 2026). They were generated by the example script in GitHub Actions run [40713845240](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/actions/runs/40713845240), as recorded in [the example provenance](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/results/PROVENANCE.md).

Inputs, results, verification values and limitations are taken from [the example README](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/README.md), [inputs.json](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/inputs.json) and [example_summary.json](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/results/example_summary.json). The heat-sink geometry is the AIPE-CAD-Agent `examples/dab-10kw` model at commit `b81bf69`, as recorded in [the CAD source note](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/examples/dab_10kw_heatsink/cad/SOURCE.md). Benchmark accuracy is taken from [the validation record](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Field-Simulation/blob/main/docs/validation.md), and the workflow role from [the AIPE-Workflows DAB workflow](https://github.com/FulongLi/AIPE-Workflows/blob/main/workflows/converter_dab/README.md), accessed on 29 October 2026.

The earlier example on this page, a heat sink for two TO-247 devices (`examples/heatsink_to247`), was replaced by the DAB example on 29 October 2026, and its three figures were removed from the site.
