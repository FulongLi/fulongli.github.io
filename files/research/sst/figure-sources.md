# Solid-state transformer figure sources

All three figures were drawn for the website on 29 October 2026 in the shared site palette; no figure is copied from the source documents.

- `/images/research/sst/sst-stage-configurations.svg` summarises the single-, two- and three-stage SST configurations as classified in Fulong Li's ASSIST WP6 review (2022), by the number of DC links.
- `/images/research/sst/dab-phase-shift.svg` shows idealised phase-shift waveforms computed from equal-amplitude square waves with a phase shift of 0.12 of the switching period. It illustrates the principle and does not use any design data.
- `/images/research/sst/dab-inductor-window.svg` places the stated limits from Fulong Li's DAB design report for the Cardiff University collaboration (February 2026) on a logarithmic axis: 28 µH and 1.1 mH bounds, and 250 µH selected.

The design values quoted on the page (15 kW rating, 7.5 kW minimum, 375 W and 37 W power steps, phase shift, RMS currents, conduction losses, 99.84% conduction-only efficiency and the 32 W ZVS limit) are taken from Section 2.2.5 of that report.
